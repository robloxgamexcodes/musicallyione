CapCut edits video files stored on your phone, so to work on a TikTok clip you first need that clip as a file. This guide shows the simple route: save it as an MP4, then import it.

> **Quick answer:** Save the public TikTok as an MP4, then open CapCut, start a new project and pick the file from your gallery. Only edit videos you made or have permission to reuse.

---

## Before you start: permission

Editing someone else's video does not make it yours. Reusing a clip, even after changes, can break the creator's rights and TikTok's terms. Use your own videos, or get permission and credit the creator. Our [responsible use note](https://musicaldown.se/musically-down/) explains what the tool is meant for.

## Step 1: Save the TikTok as an MP4

1. In TikTok, tap **Share**, then **Copy link**.
2. Paste the link into the box on the [TikTok video downloader](https://musicaldown.se/) and press Download.
3. Choose the **MP4** option. If several sizes are listed, pick the highest one, because that is the sharpest copy the video offers.
4. Save the file. If you are unsure where it went, see the guides for [iPhone](https://musicaldown.se/download-tiktok-iphone/) and [Android](https://musicaldown.se/download-tiktok-android/).

---

## Step 2: Import it into CapCut

1. Open CapCut and start a **new project**.
2. Choose the saved video from your gallery. On iPhone it must be in Photos; on Android it is usually in your gallery or Downloads.
3. Add it to the timeline and start editing.

Menu labels in CapCut change between versions, so look for the option to create a project and import from your device.

---

## Step 3: Edit without losing quality

- **Start from the best file.** Editing cannot add detail that was never there. See [why a saved TikTok can look blurry](https://musicaldown.se/blog/tiktok-video-quality-explained/).
- **Keep it vertical.** TikTok videos are usually 9:16, so keep that ratio unless you have a reason to change it.
- **Export at the same size or lower.** Exporting larger does not make a clip sharper, it only makes the file bigger.
- **Avoid screen recordings.** They add another round of compression.

## FAQ

### Does CapCut accept the MP4 file?
CapCut usually imports common video formats such as MP4. If a file will not import, try saving it again at another option from the list.

### Why can I not find the saved video on my phone?
On iPhone, check the Safari downloads list or the Files app, then save it to Photos. On Android, check the Downloads folder.

### Why is the video blurry in CapCut?
It was probably blurry in the source or was compressed again. See the quality guide above.

### Can I use the TikTok sound in CapCut?
You can import a saved audio file, but check the music rights before you publish.
