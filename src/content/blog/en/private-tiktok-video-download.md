You found a TikTok you want to keep, pasted the link into a downloader, and it failed. If the video is private, that is expected. Here is why it happens and what you can do about it.

> **Quick answer:** No. A private TikTok can only be watched by its owner and people the owner has approved, so a downloader that works from a public link has nothing to fetch. MusicallyDown does not download private, deleted or restricted videos, and any tool that claims otherwise deserves suspicion.

---

## Why private videos cannot be downloaded

- **They are not public.** A private video is only served to accounts with permission. A link alone does not open it for everyone else.
- **MusicallyDown only reads public links.** It never asks for your TikTok username or password, so it has no way to see private content.
- **"Unlock private videos" sites are a risk.** A tool that asks for your login to get around privacy can take over your account. Never share your password with a downloader.

---

## What you can do instead

### 1. It is your own video
Open it in TikTok and use the app's own save option, or request your account data from TikTok's settings. Menu names change, so check the current app. Our guide to [backing up your own TikTok videos](https://musicaldown.se/blog/back-up-your-tiktok-videos/) covers the options.

### 2. It belongs to someone else
Ask the creator to send you the file, or to make the video public and allow downloads. If they say no, respect that.

### 3. It used to work, but now fails
The creator may have switched the video to private, deleted it, or limited who can see it. Open the link again to confirm it still plays.

### 4. It is public but still will not download
Then the problem is something else, such as an expired link or a temporary error. Go through the [8 checks for a TikTok that will not download](https://musicaldown.se/blog/tiktok-video-not-downloading-fixes/).

## How to check whether a video is public

Open the link in a private or incognito browser window where you are not logged in to TikTok.

- **It plays:** the video is public. Paste the link into the [TikTok video downloader](https://musicaldown.se/) and pick an option.
- **It asks you to log in, or says the video is unavailable:** it is private, removed or restricted, and cannot be saved this way.

## FAQ

### Is there a private TikTok downloader that really works?
Not a trustworthy one. Real privacy settings are enforced by TikTok. Tools that claim to bypass them usually want your password, show ads, or do nothing.

### Will MusicallyDown ask for my TikTok password?
Never. If any site asks, close it.

### Why does a video say "unavailable" instead of private?
It can mean the video was deleted, is restricted in your region, or the creator changed who can view it.

### Can I download my own private video?
You can usually save it from inside TikTok while logged in to your account. A link-based downloader cannot see it.
