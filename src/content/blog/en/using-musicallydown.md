## How to download a TikTok video with MusicallyDown

The whole process takes about ten seconds.

### Step 1: Copy the TikTok video link

Open the video in the TikTok app (or in your browser), tap **Share**, then tap **Copy link**.

### Step 2: Paste the link into MusicallyDown

Go to the [MusicallyDown TikTok downloader](https://musicaldown.se/), paste the link into the input box and press **Download**.

### Step 3: Choose MP4 or MP3 and save

Review the options shown for your link:

- **Download MP4 (SD or HD)** saves the full video.
- **Download Audio Only (MP3)** saves just the sound.

Tap your choice and follow the download prompt. The file lands in your browser's Downloads folder.

## MusicallyDown MP4: video quality and no watermark

### Which MP4 quality will I get?

The quality you can save depends on the video that was uploaded. A downloader cannot invent detail that was never in the source. In practice:

- **SD** is usually available.
- **HD / 1080p** appears when the original was uploaded in that quality.
- **4K** is offered only when the source actually has it.

Pick the highest option listed. If HD is missing, the source video simply does not have it.

### What about the watermark?

When the results include a watermark-free version, choose the **SD or HD (no watermark)** option. Availability can change from video to video, so check the options shown for your link.

## Can you save TikToks made with CapCut?

Yes, as long as the video is **public** and you have its normal TikTok link. A TikTok that was edited in CapCut is still a regular TikTok once it is posted. Copy the link from the **Share** menu, paste it into MusicallyDown, and pick MP4 or MP3 as usual.

## Can MusicallyDown download private videos?

No. MusicallyDown works with **public** TikTok links only. If a video is private, deleted, or restricted, the tool cannot reach it, and you should not share your TikTok password with any website to try to get around that.

What you can do instead:

- Ask the creator to make the video public or to send you the file directly.
- If it is your own private video, save it from inside the TikTok app (**Share → Save video**) when that option is enabled.

You can also save public **Stories** with the [TikTok Stories downloader](https://musicaldown.se/download-tiktok-stories/).

## Link not working? Quick fixes

If a link will not process, work through this list:

1. **Check that the video is public.** Private, deleted or age/region-restricted videos cannot be processed.
2. **Copy the link again** from the Share menu. Make sure nothing was cut off and there is no extra text around it.
3. **Wait a few minutes and retry.** The video may not have been reachable the first time.
4. **Try a different browser** or turn off a VPN or ad-blocking extension that might interrupt the download.
5. **Check your storage.** A full device can stop a download from saving.

Still stuck? Visit the [FAQ](https://musicaldown.se/#faq) or [contact the team](https://musicaldown.se/contact/).
